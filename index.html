BMI Gauge – Weight Tracker: privacy policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026.

Publisher: Anwar Hussain
Privacy contact: Anwarhussain.compsci@gmail.com

Package name: com.bmi_gauge

Information stored on your device BMI Gauge stores the adult profile you enter (age, sex used by the energy equation, height and weight), activity level, weight history, optional notes, goal weight, and display preferences. It uses this information to calculate estimates and display your progress. Records stay in the app’s local storage until you remove them or uninstall the app. The app does not use accounts or developer-operated cloud synchronization. It does not send your profile or weight records to a developer-operated server.

Advertising and Google services The Android app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform to show banner ads and manage consent. The SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app and advertising interactions, and diagnostic information with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Advertising data is sent over encrypted connections. We do not pass your age, sex, height, weight, notes, goals, or other health records to the advertising SDK, and we do not sell your health records.

We request non-personalized ads. These ads may still use identifiers or local storage for measurement, frequency limiting, and fraud prevention. Where required, a consent message is shown before ads are requested. You can revisit your choices using Ad privacy choices in Settings when available. If consent or ad initialization fails, the app remains usable without ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Deleting app records does not delete data already held by Google; Google's retention and deletion practices are described in its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Learn how Google uses data from apps at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Sharing and backups If you choose Share JSON backup or Share CSV export, the app passes the selected text to the destination you choose in Android’s share sheet. You can also copy exported text manually. Backups and CSV exports contain readable personal information and are not password-protected by this app. Keep exported copies private. Other applications or storage providers handle your shared data according to their own policies. Android automatic backup is disabled for this app.

Deletion and control Use Calculators → Settings → Delete all data to remove the app’s stored profile, history, goals and preferences on this device. This action does not delete previously exported copies; remove those from their destinations yourself. Uninstalling can permanently remove locally stored history. The app cannot recover a lost backup. Restoring a backup replaces existing app records after confirmation.

Permissions and external sites This release does not request access to location, contacts, camera, device health sensors, or Health Connect. Calculation-source buttons open external websites in your browser. Those sites may receive normal browser connection information and have their own privacy policies.

Intended use The calculators are intended for adults. Do not enter children’s health information. BMI Gauge is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Changes and contact We will update this policy and its effective date when our data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the publisher using the email above.
